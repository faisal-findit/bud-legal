Bud — Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 September 2026

Bud is an expense tracker for iOS. This policy describes what data Bud collects, where it goes, and who can see it. It covers the Bud iOS app, bundle identifier com.bud.expense.tracker.

What Bud stores on your device

Bud is offline-first. Your expenses, budgets, categories and settings are written to a database on your device and stay there whether or not you are online. The app works without a network connection.

What leaves your device, and to whom

Bud uses the following third-party services. Each one receives only what is listed here.

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Firebase Authentication (Google)Your email address, and the identifier supplied by Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple if you use themTo create and sign you into your account
Cloud Firestore (Google)Your expenses, budgets, settings, and assistant conversationsTo sync your data across your devices and restore it if you reinstall
OpenAIThe text of your messages to the in-app assistant, and the contents of any receipt, screenshot, PDF or spreadsheet you submit for extractionTo answer questions and read amounts, dates and merchants out of documents
RevenueCatYour subscription status and your Bud account identifierTo determine whether your subscription is active
Apple (App Store)Your purchase, handled entirely by AppleTo process payment. Bud never sees your payment details
FrankfurterCurrency codes only — for example USD, EURTo convert amounts between currencies. No personal or financial data is sent
Please read this before submitting documents. When you send a receipt, screenshot or statement to the in-app assistant, its contents are transmitted to OpenAI for processing. Do not submit documents containing information you are not willing to share with a third-party processor.

Device permissions

Bud asks for these only when you use the feature that needs them, and it works without them:

  • Camera and photo library — to capture or select a receipt. Images are processed for expense details as described above.
  • Microphone and speech recognition — to enter an expense by voice. Speech may be transcribed using Apple's speech recognition.

What Bud does not do

  • Bud does not sell your data.
  • Bud does not use your data for advertising, and contains no advertising SDKs.
  • Bud does not connect to your bank and never asks for banking credentials.

Your data

You can sign out at any time, which stops syncing to Cloud Firestore.

Account deletion is currently handled by request. Automatic in-app deletion of your synced data is not yet available. To have your account and its stored data deleted, email us at the address below and we will action it. We are working to make this available directly in the app.

Children

Bud is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page will change. Continuing to use Bud after an update means you accept the revised policy.
